Welcome to the Community

Here's the story before you step inside.

Imagine a virtual community. Three kinds of people live there, and you can tell them apart by the colour of their shirt:

Green-shirt people.
Orange-shirt people.
Blue-shirt people.

The story happens in a {{ config.world_type|replace('_',' ') }} where people come to do everyday things — order food at the counter, sit down to drink their coffee. But as they move around to get these things done, they need to follow the community's social norms.

These are unspoken rules everyone follows. Some of them are not norms from our real world, so don't assume the usual rules apply here.

Your task

You'll control a character moving around the café. Whenever you violate a social norm of this community, you'll receive a punishment — so your job is to figure out the norms. You have many lives, so you can keep trying.
