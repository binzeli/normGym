Part 2 of 4 · The characters

How to read the characters

Three colours. Characters appear in orange, green, and blue, which represents three different social groups.
“You”. You can be in any of the three social groups. Your own character is labelled You and carries a gold star.

A character's status

Badges above a character show what it is doing right now.

Part 3 of 4 · When you break a norm

Signals of a norm violation

If you violate a norm, you will see a window pop up, and a red cross mark appears on the character who violated the norm.

Part 4 of 4 · Your task

⚠️ Your task

1. Move around and reach the goal locations.

enter the café  →  navigate to your assigned goal position  →  leave the café through a door

You get your goals one at a time: once you finish your first goal, the next one appears. Your current goal cell is outlined and labelled GOAL on the board, so you never have to search for it.

2. Figure out the social norm while you reach your goal.

We will not tell you what the social norm is; you have to work it out yourself from the feedback.

The same red ✗ also appears on other characters when they break the norm, so watch them too.

Scoring

+10reach your goal
−5collision with another character
−20breaking the social norm
−0.1each step
Your aim is to score as many points as possible. Reach your goal quickly, avoid collisions, and follow the social norm.
